# Content Blueprint

## Strategy
Present clear confirmed services, authentic project evidence, and an easy enquiry path. Avoid keyword stuffing and generic filler.

## Search intent hypotheses (validate before production)
- home renovation contractor Mayur Vihar
- home renovation East Delhi
- interior work Mayur Vihar Phase III
- kitchen renovation East Delhi
- bathroom renovation East Delhi
- full-home renovation Delhi

## Home `/`
Purpose: explain the offer, build trust, show imagery, guide visitors to services/contact.
H1 draft: “Thoughtful Renovation. Better Everyday Living.”
Intro draft: “Explore renovation and interior-work possibilities for your home. Share your requirements to discuss the right next step for your space.”
CTAs: “Discuss Your Project”; “Explore Projects”.
Sections: hero, introduction, confirmed services, approved projects, verified value points, confirmed process, verified FAQ, contact CTA.

## Services `/services`
Only confirmed services. Explain scope, relevant approved image, common considerations, enquiry CTA, related links. No unsupported price promises or guarantees.

## Projects `/projects`
Use approved real work for production. Concept placeholders must be labeled illustrative. Do not invent captions, client names, locations, or before/after comparisons.

## About `/about`
No invented biography, years in business, credentials, team size, or company history. Use approved facts only.

## Contact `/contact`
Confirmed phone, approved WhatsApp if available, confirmed service area, and a demo form that transmits/stores nothing.

## Draft metadata
Title: `Home Renovation & Interior Work in East Delhi | Duggu`
Description: `Explore home renovation and interior-work options in East Delhi. View project examples and contact Duggu to discuss your requirements.`
Confirm business name/services/area before publishing.

## FAQs to verify
Which services are available? Which areas are served? How are estimates requested? What should customers prepare? What is the work process? Are site visits or estimates charged?
Do not invent answers.

## Content QA
One clear H1 per page, natural location terms, no fake testimonials, no unverified claims, accurate image labels, no keyword stuffing.
