## 3. Evidence and unknowns
Screenshots show customer comments about a bathroom renovation with modern fittings, stylish tiles, and finishing, and a kitchen renovation praising professionalism, attention to detail, and the result. These are research signals, not permission to republish testimonials.

Owner confirmation required: exact business name, full service list, service area, free/paid consultations, approved project photos, process, warranties, prices, timelines, materials, official contact/WhatsApp, hours, and whether an official standalone website exists.

## 4. Goals and exclusions
Goals: polished private prototype, clear services/gallery, SEO-ready structure, easy contact path, maintainable React/TypeScript code, minimal AI iterations.
Excluded: backend, database, accounts, admin panel, payments, booking, automated quotes, CRM, production launch, domain connection, public indexing, promises of rankings or leads.

## 5. Audience
Primary: homeowners in East Delhi considering renovation/interior work. Secondary: kitchen/bathroom project customers only if those services are confirmed.
Visitors need to know which jobs are handled, see genuine examples, check service area, understand how to enquire, and know what to prepare before requesting an estimate.

## 6. Proposed sitemap
- `/` Home
- `/services` Services
- `/projects` Projects gallery
- `/about` About
- `/contact` Contact

If the existing project suits a single-page design, equivalent sections with working anchor navigation are acceptable. Avoid unnecessary routing dependencies.

## 7. Homepage structure
1. Header: typographic wordmark, navigation, contact CTA.
2. Hero with clear positioning, CTA buttons, licensed illustrative imagery.
3. Short introduction.
4. Confirmed service cards.
5. Featured project gallery.
6. Proposed value points based on review themes, without guarantees.
7. Proposed work process, subject to owner confirmation.
8. FAQ with verified answers only.
9. Contact CTA.
10. Footer with confirmed information.

Provisional headline: **“Thoughtful Renovation. Better Everyday Living.”**
Provisional intro: “Explore renovation and interior-work possibilities for your home. Share your requirements to discuss the right next step for your space.”
CTAs: “Discuss Your Project” and “Explore Projects”.

Candidate service categories for layout only: home renovation, kitchen renovation, bathroom renovation, interior improvements. Confirm before publication.

## 8. Supporting pages
**Services:** confirmed service descriptions, scope, approved photos, useful FAQs, enquiry CTA. Do not create thin pages only for keywords.
**Projects:** responsive gallery, approved real photos for production, no fabricated before/after or project histories.
**About:** no invented owner bio, years of experience, certifications, or team size.
**Contact:** confirmed phone, WhatsApp only if approved, service area, demo form.

Add sitemap, robots configuration, and structured data only when domain and facts are confirmed. No fake ratings/review markup. Keep private concept out of search indexing. SEO does not guarantee rankings or enquiries.

## 10. Technical stack
Prefer the existing React + TypeScript + Vite setup and styling approach. Inspect before editing. Reuse components; do not add backend, database, auth, payments, or heavy animation libraries.

Suggested components: Header, MobileNavigation, HeroSection, ServiceCard, ServicesSection, ProjectCard, ProjectGallery, ProcessSection, FAQAccordion, ContactCTA, ContactFormDemo, Footer.

## 11. Accessibility and performance
Responsive mobile/tablet/desktop; semantic landmarks; keyboard navigation and visible focus; accessible form labels; adequate contrast; reduced-motion support; no horizontal overflow; optimized images; lazy-load below-fold images; avoid unnecessary scripts. Report actual test results, not assumed scores.

## 12. Demo form
No backend, network requests, or data persistence. Validate locally and display:
“This is a concept demo. Your details have not been sent. Contact the business directly to discuss your project.”
Never show a false success message.

## 13. Privacy and disclosure
Keep private or share only as a clearly labeled concept. Do not impersonate the company, claim endorsement, publish unapproved photos/reviews, collect personal data, or enable public indexing before authorization.

## 14. Acceptance criteria
- Consistent professional visual system.
- Responsive layouts.
- All links/CTAs work.
- Reusable service/gallery components.
- No placeholder presented as real project.
- No unverified claims.
- Demo form sends/stores nothing.
- SEO metadata and semantic structure exist.
- Production build succeeds.
- Concept clearly unofficial and private/permissioned.
- Owner can understand the concept in a short demo.

Success means a credible, tested private prototype and a professional pitch. Rankings, traffic, and leads can only be measured after an authorized launch.
